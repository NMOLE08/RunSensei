## Source interpretation

- [NHS Couch to 5K](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/get-running-with-couch-to-5k/couch-to-5k-running-plan/) supports beginner timed run/walk structure, three sessions, rest days, and five-minute walking warm-up/cooldown. It does not validate either RunSensei 12-week programme or the numerical comparators above.
- [Berkshire Healthcare return-to-running guidance](https://www.berkshirehealthcare.nhs.uk/advice/return-to-running-programme) supplies context for a 30-minute walk and two-to-three sessions with rest. Its clinical context and individualisation warning mean it cannot be treated as automatic eligibility.
- [Running injury/training parameter systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34478518/) reports inconsistent associations for specific training parameters. It supports caution; it does not establish universal thresholds.

## Programme-table review required separately

Entry rules do **not** approve the selected workouts. The reviewer must separately approve or replace both twelve-week tables, their recovery weeks, progression, completion-event structure, effort wording, stage repeat/regress rules, interruption handling, and feedback rules.
