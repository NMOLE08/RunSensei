# Torto product direction — Phase 0

## Product identity

Torto is the application. Sensei is Torto’s personalized running-coaching experience. Sensei is not a third character.

Visible product copy uses **Torto** for the app, account, settings, integrations, and reminders. Existing technical identities such as `com.aisensei.app`, the `aisensei` URL scheme, existing authentication storage keys, SQLite identifiers, and historical `runsensei.*` policy references remain unchanged for upgrade and provenance compatibility.

## Two mentors

Torto has two mentors:

- **Moss — The Hard-to-Impress Mentor:** calm, composed, observant, demanding, patient, and restrained with praise.
- **Jett — The Roasting Mentor:** playful, witty, energetic, lightly teasing, and never humiliating or shaming.

Moss and Jett present the same approved coaching decisions differently. Coach selection does not alter eligibility, safety decisions, workouts, schedules, pace targets, duration, progression, recovery, policy authorization, or plan publication.

Both mentors use direct and respectful language for pain, illness, distress, uncertainty, disability, rest, missed sessions, and sensitive situations. The current introduction recordings do not have verified transcripts. The app therefore does not display invented subtitles or claim that recorded dialogue has been reviewed against these persona definitions.

## Coaching rollout

The intended product order is:

1. Beginner 5K internal pilot.
2. Personalized 10K coaching.
3. Professionally reviewed personalized half-marathon coaching.
4. Professionally reviewed personalized full-marathon coaching.

This order is roadmap metadata. It cannot authorize a plan, a policy, or a workout. Runtime plan status, policy authorization, runner evidence, assessment, and account ownership continue to decide whether a plan is available.

## Current capability boundary

Beginner 5K has research, preview, evidence, and guarded publication infrastructure. Its research-only previews remain distinct from active plans. Initial publication requires verified exact-version release authorization, and next-week progression remains blocked until a reviewed transition rule exists.

10K does not have a complete authorized coaching pathway. Half-marathon work is a draft research simulation. Full-marathon coaching is not implemented as a production pathway. The existing habit goal remains available according to its current application flow.

## Calendar-only availability

Newer calendar-only coaching pathways use training frequency, available weekdays, a preferred longest-workout day, and dated exceptions. They do not require a daily maximum-minutes answer or fabricate a default time allowance. Older policy/history contracts remain historical records and must not be silently rewritten.

## Phase 0 scope

Phase 0 establishes consistent product language, authoritative mentor personas, deterministic mentor presentation, and honest rollout messaging. It does not add a database migration, training algorithms, 10K/half/full-marathon generation, policy trust configuration, reviewer approvals, autonomous adaptation, new mascot animation systems, speech synthesis, or an AI conversation system.
