# Source register (Phase 0)

Retrieved 2026-10-22. These sources document public behavior or general guidance. They are evidence only: they do not grant permission to reproduce a plan, reveal private algorithms, or override this project's contract.

| Source | Public finding used | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
