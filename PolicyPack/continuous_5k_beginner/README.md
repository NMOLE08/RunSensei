# Continuous-Running Beginner 5K Pack — Draft v0.1.0

This folder is a separate, source-controlled **draft-only** programme. It is not a reviewed coaching plan, not medical advice, and cannot be activated for production.

## Evidence references

- [NHS Couch to 5K](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/get-running-with-couch-to-5k/couch-to-5k-running-plan/) supports general beginner run/walk structure, three weekly sessions, rest days, walking warm-up/cool-down and self-paced effort. It is a nine-week programme and does **not** validate this pack’s 12-week continuous-running table.
- [NHS running-injury guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/knee-pain-and-other-running-injuries/) supports gradual build-up, warm-up/cool-down and stopping if something feels wrong. It does not supply a universal numerical progression rule.
- [Running-injury training-parameter systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34478518/) reports conflicting evidence for individual training-parameter and injury relationships. It must not be converted into a universal percentage rule.

## Explicit exclusions

- No target pace, heart-rate zone, or GPS-derived prescription.
- No conversion from minutes to distance.
- No interval, threshold, hill, or speed session.
- No automatic progression, repeat-stage, regression, or return-after-interruption rule.
- No production activation before qualified running-coach and safety review.

## Review worksheet

A qualified reviewer must approve or replace: continuous-running eligibility, starting-workload criteria, all timed sessions, deload timing, event readiness, feedback-based progression, stage repetition/regression, interruption re-entry, and clinical escalation language.
